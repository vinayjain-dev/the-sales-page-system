# Start here: five prompts to try

Paste any of these into Claude after you have installed the skills (see `README.md`). Replace the example details with
your own. Claude will ask for anything you leave out.

---

## 1. The basic one

> Build me a sales page for my business.

Claude interviews you first (offer, buyer, price, proof, guarantee, brand, builder), then writes and builds the whole
page as one HTML file. Answer "not yet" to anything you do not have.

---

## 2. A course, with the details filled in

> Build me a sales page for my online course "Excel for Small Business Owners." It is six short modules, $297 in one
> payment, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. My buyers are owners who spend hours on spreadsheets every week. I
> have three real testimonials I can share. Use an ivory and ink look. I will paste it into Kajabi.

Claude uses the editorial palette, writes the beats in order, and gives you the Kajabi paste steps and a list of the
placeholders you still need to fill.

---

## 3. A local service, with a bold look

> I run a roofing company in Denver. Build me a sales page for my free roof inspection, which I offer for a
> limited number of homes each month. Bold and industrial look. I have no testimonials yet, so use honest placeholders.
> I will paste it into WordPress.

Claude uses the bold industrial type pairing, leaves the testimonials as clear placeholders instead of inventing them, and
removes any fake scarcity it cannot back.

---

## 4. The follow-up emails

> Write the five follow-up emails for buyers who did not take my $47 upgrade. The upgrade is a step-by-step
> video course. Use the same voice as my sales page. My guarantee is 30 days.

Claude uses the upsell follow-up template from the copywriting skill, one email per day, one link repeated two or three
times, and the guarantee in the P.S.

---

## 5. Product images for the page

> Make me prompts for three product images for my sales page: a black presentation box with four frosted glass cards
> rising out of it, a single glass card for my bonus, and a guarantee seal with no text. I will generate them in Higgsfield.
> Give me the settings too.

Claude uses the Higgsfield design guide: the product render templates, the settings for each image, and the WebP steps
for when the images are ready.

---

## After the first page

- Ask for a new headline and a new angle: "Write three new headlines for this page, one for each type of buyer."
- Ask for an ad: "Write a 4:5 feed ad for this offer, in the same voice, with one command."
- Ask for a review: "Check this page against the build checklist and tell me what is missing."
